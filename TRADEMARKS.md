- Use them to identify **your own** product, service or organisation.

That last point is the whole reason for this file. The artwork is licensed
permissively on purpose — reuse it, remix it, ship it. But a brand mark works by
telling people *who made this*, and a mark anybody may apply to anything has
stopped doing the one job it has. Reserving the marks is what makes the rest of
the estate safe to give away.

If you are unsure whether a use is covered, the answer is usually yes for
describing us and no for labelling yourself.
